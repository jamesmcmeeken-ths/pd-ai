### **DRRi** (Directly Responsible Individual)
Person responsible for a product area who ensures work continues even when they're on leave.

### **Cross-functional Partner**
Stakeholders from other departments (product, marketing, analytics) who collaborate with engineering.

## Performance Calibration Axes

### **Project Impact**
Direct contributions toward team goals and deliverables.

### **Engineering Excellence**
Contributions to codebase quality, observability, helping teams move faster, catching bugs earlier.

### **Direction**
Setting priorities, unblocking cross-functional partners, strategic contributions.

### **People**
Mentorship, guidance, helping others achieve more through knowledge sharing.

## AI / Development Tools

### **OpenCode**
AI coding assistant used for productivity, code generation, and codebase analysis.

### **Skills**
Custom AI instruction sets that automate specific workflows (e.g., processing meeting notes, quarterly transitions).

### **MCP** (Model Context Protocol)
Protocol for connecting AI assistants to external tools like Jira, Notion, Slack, and GitHub.

## Development Process Terms

### **Lead Time**
Total time from idea to production deployment.

### **Inner/Outer Loop**
- **Inner Loop**: Time developers spend in local development environment
- **Outer Loop**: Time from commit to production

### **Ticket Quality**
Well-defined units of work with clear context, requirements, and acceptance criteria.

### **TDD** (Test-Driven Development)
Development approach where tests are written before implementation code.
