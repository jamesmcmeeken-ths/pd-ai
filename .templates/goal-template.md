## Tips for Setting Goals

### SMART Goals
- **Specific**: Clear and unambiguous
- **Measurable**: Quantifiable or verifiable
- **Achievable**: Realistic given time and resources
- **Relevant**: Aligned with team/company objectives
- **Time-bound**: Has a clear deadline

### Calibration Axes
Use the four axes (Project Impact, Engineering Excellence, Direction, People) to ensure balanced goals across all areas of contribution.

### Evidence-Based
Frame goals with measurable outcomes:
- "Decrease lead time by X%"
- "Ship Y features"
- "Reduce error rate by Z%"
- "Help team identify N pivots through confidence assessments"

### Link to Team Goals
Align personal goals with team objectives:
- Team needs to ship X → I will contribute by doing Y
- Team struggles with Z → I will improve it through W

### Review Cadence
- Weekly: Check progress in one-to-ones
- Monthly: Assess overall trajectory
- End of quarter: Full review and retrospective
